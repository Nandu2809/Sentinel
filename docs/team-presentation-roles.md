# Sentinel — Team Presentation Roles & Responsibility Matrix

## Overview
This document structures team presentation responsibilities for live hackathon presentation decks and live demo operators.

---

## 👥 Role Division Matrix

### Role 1: Presenter 1 — Pitch Lead (Problem & Product Story)
- **Responsibility**: Introduces problem, $C_{\text{FP}}$ vs $C_{\text{FN}}$ business dilemma, Track 2 positioning, and core 30-second pitch.
- **Key Deliverables**: Slides 1, 2, 3, 10.

### Role 2: Presenter 2 — Technical & AI Lead (Architecture & Graph Intelligence)
- **Responsibility**: Explains Java 21 microservices, Kafka streaming pipeline, 34-feature extraction, and SVG relationship topology graph.
- **Key Deliverables**: Slides 4, 5, 6.

### Role 3: Presenter 3 — Demo Operator (Live Command Center Demonstration)
- **Responsibility**: Executes live demo of Scenario A (`APPROVE`), Scenario B (`REVIEW`), Scenario C (`BLOCK`), Explainable AI panel, and SOC Incident bridge.
- **Key Deliverables**: Slides 7, 8.

### Role 4: Presenter 4 — Business & Security Lead (Q&A Defense Lead)
- **Responsibility**: Explains business value, anti-leakage scientific discipline, synthetic metric disclosures, and leads technical/business Q&A defense.
- **Key Deliverables**: Slide 9 & Q&A Master Database.

---

## 👤 Solo Presenter Adaptation (Single Presenter)
If presenting solo:
- **00:00–01:00**: Problem + Solution + Architecture (Combine Roles 1 & 2).
- **01:00–04:00**: Live Demo (Scenarios A, B, C + Explainable AI + Cost Simulator) (Role 3).
- **04:00–05:00**: Business Value + Disclosures + Q&A (Role 4).
