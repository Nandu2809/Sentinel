# Sentinel — The 30-Second "Why Sentinel?" Pitch Statement

## Pitch Statement

> *"Sentinel does not simply ask:
> **'Is this transaction fraud?'**
> 
> It asks:
> **'How risky is this transaction,**
> **why is it risky,**
> **what entity relationships surround it,**
> **what will it cost the business if we are wrong,**
> **and what should the SOC team do next?'**
> 
> By uniting in-memory relationship graph topology, asymmetric business cost optimization, and SOC incident workflows, Sentinel transforms raw payment telemetry into actionable financial security decisions."*
